Offline Crop & Pest Quick Guide

Rice (Palay)

Ideal water level: 2–5 cm during vegetative stage. Drain field 7-10 days before harvest.

Corn (Mais)

Requires well-drained loam soil. Sensitive to waterlogging during early germination.

Tomato (Kamatis)

Needs consistent watering at the base to prevent blossom-end rot. Stake plants early.

Eggplant (Talong)

Watch out for shoot and fruit borers. Prune lower yellow leaves for better airflow.
